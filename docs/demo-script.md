# Sidecar demonstration script

Target: approximately two minutes of narration over an actual screen recording. This is a recording plan, not a completed video. Use the public app at https://sidecar-qloo.onrender.com/. Keep credentials and hosting dashboards out of frame. Check the event's final video requirements before publishing.

## Recording setup

Wake the free host before recording. Use a new blank brief, with business name empty, bookstore as the business, Austin, Texas as the city and cafe as the desired partner. Use the default collaboration objective. Resolve Haruki Murakami as an author, Khruangbin as an artist, and the 1995 Before Sunrise film. Confirm Coffee shop (place category).

Record real interactions. Edit out waiting periods if necessary and add a small "wait shortened" caption. Do not portray edited elapsed time as measured performance. Results can change; adjust narration to what actually appears. The October 6 run is documented in live-results-2026-10-06.md.

## 0:00-0:15 | The decision

Show the public workspace and starting brief.

"A local bookstore wants to host a neighborhood event. Finding a nearby cafe is easy. Finding a collaboration that fits its character takes more work. Sidecar turns a business's cultural references into partner research and a concrete idea to investigate."

## 0:15-0:40 | Resolve real references

Show reference search and confirmation, especially the two Before Sunrise results. Confirm the 1995 film and the place-category tag.

"We start with books, music and film: Haruki Murakami, Khruangbin and Before Sunrise. These are real Qloo entities, not keywords invented by the model. When names are ambiguous, the owner confirms the intended match. Then we choose the kind of business we want to meet."

## 0:40-1:00 | Follow the evidence

Run research. Show the live label, Palomino Coffee, source evidence and request parameters. Expand Research activity if demonstrating the planner's action.

"Qloo discovers places using those references, the Austin area and our category. Sidecar preserves the provider's ordering and evidence. The bounded planner can decide whether another analysis is useful; in this run it finishes after discovery. The affinity score is a taste relationship, not a prediction of sales or proof of shared customers."

## 1:00-1:20 | Revise the decision

Exclude The Yard if present. Show What changed and the replacement candidate. Use the actual replacement name if different.

"This mixed-use venue is not the cafe we had in mind, so we exclude it. Sidecar shows exactly what changed. Here, The Yard disappears, Mozart's enters the shortlist, and the original discovery evidence is reused with its original timestamp. The owner's constraint changes the result."

## 1:20-1:45 | Make a reviewable plan

Select Palomino Coffee, generate the proposal, edit its title, show verification questions and download Evidence.

"Now we turn one candidate into an editable collaboration brief. It includes an event concept, next steps, questions about capacity and costs, and a measurement plan. Generated ideas stay separate from confirmed facts. The export includes the source record, so someone else can inspect the basis for the proposal."

## 1:45-2:05 | State the evidence and next step

Show the public repository and live report, or close on the app with the URL.

"Sidecar is live on free hosting, with server-side credentials and a persistent total spending cap. We tested three fixed briefs and recorded model-only comparisons, including failures. Category and geographic fit still need human checks. Our next step is owner feedback: does this help a real business choose a conversation worth starting?"
